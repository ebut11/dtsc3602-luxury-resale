Understand the data & limitations

Retention = listing asking price ÷ Rebag estimated retail price × 100. Retail estimates may reflect current retail prices rather than the original purchase price. Age was decoded for Louis Vuitton and Chanel only. Accessories are blank in 44% of listings, and the 68 watches are too few for a separate analysis. This demo therefore does not offer watches.

Older unsold listings may distort asking-price retention. The research will compare recent September–October 2026 listings against the broader collection. The demo uses illustrative constants and feature adjustments; it has no trained prediction endpoint and does not report model performance. Preview brands are limited to the three explicitly named in the proposal.
