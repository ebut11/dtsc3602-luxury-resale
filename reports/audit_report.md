# D2 Data Audit: Completeness and Representativeness

Generated by `scripts/audit_checks.py` from `data/clean/`. Counts use all 15,863 cleaned rows; figures that use `asking_price_ratio` values use the 12,557 training rows only (1,839 with a target), so the reserved test set stays unseen.

The target is Rebag's **advertised asking price** divided by its **estimated retail price**. These are listing prices, not completed sales.

## 3. Summary of concerns

- **Small target set:** 14.5% of rows have a target; coverage is uneven across brands (see 1b).
- **Retail price comes only from partner listings:** all 2,303 rows with a target are partner (dropship) listings; Rebag's own listings never show a retail price. Results describe partner listings.
- **Selection:** rows with a target are more often handbags and less often Pristine or jewelry (see 1b and 1c), so they are not a random subset of all listings.
- **Age only for two brands:** Louis Vuitton and Chanel; microchipped items give only a maximum age.
- **Concentration:** Louis Vuitton, Gucci and Chanel dominate; watches and several SouthPark stores are absent or too small.
- **One reseller, one day:** all rows are Rebag listings on October 20, 2026; partner (dropship) listings are about half the data.
- **Asking prices, not sales:** older listings likely include markdowns, and the ratio is capped at 83.3%.
